# Privacy Policy
Last Updated: 2025/04/10

This Privacy Policy explains how Sevinci (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and protects your personal data in connection with our digital services.

We comply with the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)** for clients in the EU and applicable Swiss data protection laws.

---

## 1. Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum personal data necessary to provide our services. This includes:

- **Contact Information**: Name, email address, phone number (for invoicing and communication).
- **Billing Details**: Company name, VAT number, payment status, and invoicing address.
- **Project Content**: Any files, text, or images you send us for your website or custom development.
- **Technical Metadata**: IP address, browser, and device type (used for basic security and troubleshooting).
- **Website Usage (Optional)**: Anonymous analytics (page views, referrers), if enabled.

We do **not** use tracking cookies or personalized ads unless explicitly enabled and agreed.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Data

We use personal data only to:

- Provide and maintain our services
- Process payments and invoices
- Communicate project progress, updates, and service changes
- Ensure website security and platform functionality
- Comply with legal or regulatory obligations

We **do not sell or share** your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

---

## 3. Legal Basis (GDPR)

We process your personal data under the following lawful bases:

| Purpose | Legal Basis |
|--------|-------------|
| Service delivery | Contractual necessity |
| Communication | Legitimate interest |
| Billing & invoicing | Legal obligation |
| Analytics & platform logs | Legitimate interest |
| Data retention | Legal obligation / Legitimate interest |

---

## 4. Data Sharing

We may share data with trusted service providers **only when necessary** for operations:

- **Payment processors** (e.g., Stripe, PayPal)
- **Hosting providers** (e.g., Kreativmedia)
- **Email delivery services** (for transactional messages)

These providers are GDPR-compliant and contractually bound to data protection standards.

---

## 5. Data Retention

- Project-related files: Retained for up to **12 months after contract completion**, unless otherwise requested.
- Billing & legal records: Retained for **10 years** (as required under Swiss law).
- Communication history: Retained for a maximum of **3 years** after last contact.

You may request early deletion unless required for legal or contractual reasons.

---

## 6. Your Rights (EU/CH)

If you are located in the **EU** or **Switzerland**, you have the right to:

- Access your personal data
- Correct or update inaccurate data
- Request deletion (“right to be forgotten”)
- Object to or restrict processing
- Data portability (receive your data in a machine-readable format)
- File a complaint with a data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email: **[privacy@sevinci.ch]**

---

## 7. Data Security

We take appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

- SSL encryption across all websites and communication channels
- Access control and authentication for internal systems
- Regular software updates and security patching
- Minimal data collection and limited retention

However, no digital system is 100% secure. In case of a breach, we will notify affected users and authorities as required by law.

---

## 8. International Transfers

All data is hosted in **Switzerland** or the **EU**. If data is ever processed outside the EU/CH, we will ensure equivalent protection through:

- Data Processing Agreements (DPAs)
- Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)

---

## 9. Third-Party Tools

We may use third-party tools that process limited personal data:

- Payment tools (e.g., Stripe, PayPal)
- Hosting (e.g., Kreativmedia)
- CMS systems (e.g., Corazon CMS)

Each tool is vetted for GDPR/Swiss compliance and data handling standards.
